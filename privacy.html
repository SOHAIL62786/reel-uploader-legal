Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Reel Uploader application ("the App") accesses, uses, and stores data in connection with your TikTok account.

1. Data Accessed

When you authorize the App via TikTok's OAuth login, the App may access the following, strictly within the scopes you approve:

  • Basic profile information (such as your TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar) via user.info.basic.
  • Permission to publish video content directly to your TikTok profile via video.publish.
  • Permission to upload video content as a draft via video.upload.
  • Performance analytics for videos published through the App (views, likes, comments, shares) via video.list.

2. How Data Is Used

Data accessed through the App is used solely to perform the actions you request: uploading/publishing video content to your own account and retrieving analytics for that content. The App does not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to these functions.

3. Data Storage

Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok are stored locally by the App for the sole purpose of maintaining your authenticated session between uploads. These tokens are not shared with, sold to, or accessible by any third party.

4. Data Sharing

The App does not share, sell, or transfer any data obtained from TikTok to third parties. Data is used exclusively between your TikTok account and the App itself.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You may revoke the App's access at any time via your TikTok account's connected apps settings. Revoking access invalidates the App's stored tokens. You may also request deletion of any locally stored data associated with your account by contacting the developer.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the minimum age required to hold a TikTok account.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in the App's functionality or data practices. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to request data deletion, please reach out via the contact details on the developer's GitHub profile.
